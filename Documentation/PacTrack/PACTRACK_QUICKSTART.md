# PacTrack Quick Start Guide

## What is PacTrack?
PacTrack is your personal RooPAC (Roo Physical Activity Credits) graduation requirement calculator. It helps you plan and track your physical activity credits to ensure you meet Abington Friends Upper School's graduation requirements.

## Credit Requirements by Grade
- **9th Grade**: 5 credits required
- **10th Grade**: 5 credits required  
- **11th Grade**: 4 credits required
- **12th Grade**: 3 credits required

## How to Use PacTrack

### Step 1: Select Your Grade
At the top of the screen, select your current grade (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th). This sets your target credit requirement.

### Step 2: Plan Your Activities

The screen shows three seasonal slots:

**Fall Slot Options:**
- Varsity/JV Sports (3 credits)
- Fall Play (3 credits - actor or crew)
- Robotics (satisfies entire year!)
- Yoga, Strength & Conditioning (1 credit each)

**Winter Slot Options:**
- Varsity/JV Sports (3 credits)
- Winter Musical (3 credits - actor or crew)
- Robotics (✓ if selected)
- Yoga, Strength & Conditioning (1 credit each)

**Spring Slot Options:**
- Varsity/JV Sports (3 credits)
- Fitness for Life, Dance (1 credit each)
- Team Manager, Peer Teaching (1 credit each)

### Step 3: Add Custom Credits (Optional)
Use "Additional Credits" to add:
- Outside Exemptions with custom credit values
- Other activities not listed

### Step 4: Check Your Progress
A circular progress indicator shows:
- Your current credits
- Your target for the year
- Overall progress percentage

### Step 5: Meet Your Requirement
- ✅ **Requirement Met**: If you reach your target, you'll see a success message
- ⚠️  **Short on Credits**: If you're still short, the calculator shows how many more credits you need and gives you a link to apply for an Outside RooPAC Exemption

## Special Features

### The Robotics Game-Changer
If you're doing Robotics (FRC/FTC), it **automatically satisfies your entire year's requirement** - no matter which grade you're in!

### Custom Credit Input
For Outside Exemptions or other activities, you can manually enter the credit value.

### Automatic Saving
Your plan is automatically saved every time you make a change. You can close the app and come back to the same plan.

## Tips
- Plan multiple scenarios to find the best activity combination
- Use the exemption link if you're short on credits
- Remember: **Varsity/JV Sports and Theater = 3 credits each** (the big credit earners!)
-**Session activities like Yoga = 1 credit** (good for topping off)
- **Robotics = Your entire requirement** (consider it if you're into it!)

## Need Help?
Your credit requirement is always displayed prominently. The app will tell you exactly how many credits you're short if you haven't met the requirement yet.
