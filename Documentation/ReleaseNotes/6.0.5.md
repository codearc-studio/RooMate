# RooMate 6.0.5

This update fixes how RooMate understands marker-based special schedules.

- Keeps a special school day active while a future marker is still scheduled.
- Shows the next marker, such as “School Day Ends,” during gaps with nothing scheduled.
- Prevents Today, Schedule, the menu bar, and the floating timer from saying the day is complete too early.
- Uses event-aware wording for point-in-time markers instead of treating them like duration blocks.
- Improves progress and contextual status for orientation, retreat, and other marker-heavy days.

RooMate 6.0.5 requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
