# RooMate 6.0.2

This focused reliability update makes RooMate 6 more dependable for the school day.

- Keeps the last valid Dining, Sports, Events, Clubs, announcements, and special-schedule data available during temporary outages.
- Shows a quiet last-updated or saved-data status when a refresh cannot complete.
- Opens Command-K results at the relevant class, club, event, game, dining menu, or Settings section.
- Keeps Sports focused on games while team pages are refreshed, with reminders available for individual games.
- Adds a version-aware What’s New experience and a privacy-safe feedback diagnostics preview.
- Improves floating-timer sizing for long class names and countdown values.
- Hardens RooMate’s update feed and adds repeatable release validation.

RooMate 6.0.2 requires macOS 14 or later and supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
