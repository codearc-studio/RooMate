# RooMate 6.0.1

RooMate 6 brings the school day together in one Mac app.

- A redesigned Today view for the current block, what comes next, and school announcements.
- Day, Week, and Semester Planner views for understanding the schedule at different scales.
- Dining menus, Sports game schedules, Clubs, and school Events.
- PacTrack for planning RooPAC progress locally on the Mac.
- Command-K search, the menu bar companion, and the floating timer.
- Improved notifications and more control over RooMate’s appearance and sidebar.

RooMate remains independent and is not an official school product.

Release metadata note: the public release is labeled 6.0.1, while the signed app bundle inside the published ZIP identifies itself as version 6.0, build 4.
