Questions

The quick answers.

RooMate is an independent student project built for macOS.

Is RooMate free?+

Yes. RooMate is free to download and use.

What Mac do I need?+

RooMate 6 requires macOS 14 or later and works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is RooMate an official school app?+

No. RooMate is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any school.

Does RooMate need an account?+

No. You can set up RooMate directly on your Mac without creating an account.

How do updates work?+

RooMate checks for signed updates in the app. You can also download the latest release directly from GitHub.
