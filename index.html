FAQ

A few quick things.

Is RooMate free?

Yes. RooMate is currently free to download and use.

What Mac do I need?

RooMate 6 requires macOS 14 or later and is distributed as a Universal app for Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Is RooMate official?

No. RooMate is an independent student project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any school.

What does RooMate collect?

RooMate uses limited, privacy-conscious analytics for feature usage and reliability. Custom analytics do not include names, class names, teachers, rooms, club names, team names, Dining searches, PacTrack choices, or announcement text.
